OpenWaymark Validator

Check a study bundle, or make one from a folder. Your files never leave this device: everything runs in your browser, and this page is not allowed to send anything over the network.

Check a bundle

Choose a .waymark file (a .zip works too).

Make a bundle from a folder

Choose a folder containing study.yaml and, if you have images, an assets folder. The page packs it, checks the result, and offers it for download.

Result

Passing these checks means a bundle follows the OpenWaymark format. It does not mean the bundle is safe, or that its answers are right.
